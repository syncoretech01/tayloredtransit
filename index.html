/ 06 — OPERATING PROCESS

From tender to proof of delivery.

Six controlled steps applied to every load — the same way, every time, whether it is a single tanker or a standing weekly program.

  1. Step 01

    Intake & Commodity Review

    SDS reviewed, UN number confirmed, packing group and compatibility checked against equipment options before we quote.

  2. Step 02

    Carrier Vetting

    Operating authority, $1M+ auto liability, $100K cargo, hazmat endorsement and FMCSA safety rating verified — every load, not once a year.

  3. Step 03

    Compliance & Documentation

    Shipping papers, placards, emergency response information and site-specific requirements assembled and confirmed with the driver.

  4. Step 04

    Dispatch & Execution

    Appointment set, wash certificate confirmed, hoses and fittings matched, driver briefed on both origin and destination protocols.

  5. Step 05

    In-Transit Visibility

    Tracking with proactive check calls, exception alerts and a named point of contact who already knows your load.

  6. Step 06

    Delivery & Settlement

    Signed BOL, weight tickets and wash documentation returned same-day. Clean invoice, no surprise accessorials.
